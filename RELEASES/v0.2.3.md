## Context

The last release, v0.2.1, built the system of record: every decision the platform makes is now traceable back to the research and market read that drove it. But one gap remained, and v0.2.1's notes called it out directly. The research stage was intermittently handing the analyst stage incomplete inputs, and the analyst refused to score on bad data. The result was a platform that could store a decision perfectly but had not actually made one in two weeks.

v0.2.3 closes that gap. The chain now runs the whole way through, on its own schedule, without a person in the loop, and it produced its first real investment judgments since the stall.

## Outcomes

**The platform now makes decisions unattended.** Four scheduled runs fire every weekday: a market-regime read before dawn, a pre-market research sweep, the decision-and-trade gate at the open, and a midday position check. Each runs start to finish on its own and writes its full reasoning to the system of record. The owner does not start it for it to work.

**Every name gets a real investment verdict.** On the first proven run, all three watchlist names received a full analysis: a classification (genuine investment versus speculation), an intrinsic-value estimate, and a conviction rating. The reasoning is specific, not boilerplate. For one name the analyst flagged that owner earnings were likely overstated once heavy infrastructure spending is treated as a cost of staying in business rather than growth, which pulls the fair value below today's price.

**The platform showed discipline, not just activity.** Faced with three names on a calm-market day, the analyst declined to buy all three. Each was judged a sound business, but each was trading above the price the analysis would justify, and conviction was not high enough to size a position. A platform that buys something every day is a platform with no standards. This one held its fire, which is exactly the behavior a value-investing discipline is supposed to enforce.

**Production problems are now visible in minutes.** A change to how the platform reports its own internal activity means that when a step fails, the precise reason surfaces in the operations log as readable text rather than a buried fragment. This single change turned what had been days of guesswork into same-session diagnosis, and it is the reason the final blocker was found and cleared quickly.

**The research contract is now enforced at the source.** Rather than trusting each research result to be well-formed and checking it after the fact, the platform now requires the research provider to return complete, properly-sourced, real research before the result is ever accepted. Incomplete or placeholder answers are rejected at the door. This is what unblocked the chain and what keeps it unblocked.

## What this is, and what it is not

This release proves the platform can research, value, and decide on its own. It is **not** yet a proof that the platform buys well, because it has not yet chosen to buy. The first real purchase, and the track record that follows, is the next milestone. Trading remains on paper. No real money is at risk, and that boundary is published and deliberate.

## Carrying forward

The next releases turn this from a working chain into a watchable one: a live profit-and-loss view in the owner terminal, a public log of each decision as it happens, and a broadening of the watchlist beyond the first three names. The milestone after that is the platform running clean for several consecutive trading days with no human intervention, which is the bar for calling it production-ready.
